  Filipino Sunday Worship

Worship, fellowship, and grow together with the Filipino community — a welcoming service for every Kabayan in Korea.

What to Expect

A Welcoming Worship Service

A space to worship in Filipino, connect with fellow Kabayans, and grow in fellowship together.

Praise & Worship

Filipino-language praise and worship that feels like home, wherever you are in Korea.

Word & Reflection

Weekly messages centered on faith, encouragement, and everyday life as an OFW.

Fellowship

Time to connect with fellow Filipinos before and after the service over food and conversation.

Prayer & Support

A caring community ready to pray with and support one another through life's challenges.

Open to Everyone

All Filipinos are welcome, whatever your background or how long you've been in Korea.

A Home Away From Home

A familiar, comforting space that feels like Sunday service back in the Philippines.
