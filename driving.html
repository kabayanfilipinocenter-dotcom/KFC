Step by Step

The Road to Your Driver's License

Seven stages, from registration to the day you walk out with a Korean driver's license.

1

Multiple Mock Tests

Build familiarity with written-test question patterns before sitting the real exam.

2

Traffic Safety Education (Compulsory)

A required 1-hour traffic safety education session, part of every applicant's process.

3

Health Check

A basic physical examination required before registering for the written exam.

4

Registration

Register at the driver's license examination office (운전면허시험장) for your tests.

5

Written Exam (학과시험)

The knowledge test covering traffic rules, signs, and road markings.

6

Functional Driving Test (장내기능시험)

A closed-course skills test evaluating basic vehicle control and maneuvers.

7

Actual Road Driving Exam (도로주행시험)

The final on-road test — pass this, and your license is issued.

Curriculum

What You'll Learn, Month by Month

Each month builds on the last — theory first, then vehicle control, then real road skills.

Month 1 · Weeks 1–4

Foundations of Driving & Safety

  • License application & process
  • Understanding the Korean driving test
  • Written test techniques
  • Basic traffic knowledge & road signs
  • Vehicle & motion fundamentals
  • Safety driving & defensive driving
  • Traffic violations & penalties
  • Driver dos and don'ts
  • Written exam at week 4
Month 2 · Weeks 5–8

Functional Skills

  • Vehicle familiarization & basic controls
  • Pre-driving inspection, seat & mirrors
  • Basic driving maneuvers
  • Speed zones, hill starts, direction changes
  • Lane and line control, signal operation
  • Functional test techniques & scoring
  • Guided practice & mock functional test
  • Functional driving test at week 8
Month 3 · Weeks 9–12

Actual Road Skills

  • Real-road orientation & traffic awareness
  • Lane changes, turns, U-turns, intersections
  • Traffic interaction & vulnerable road users
  • Defensive & hazard-perception driving
  • Navigation & following test routes
  • Road-test scoring & common deductions
  • Guided road practice & mock road test
  • Road driving exam at week 12

Weekly Mock Test Requirement

Throughout Month 1, students complete 7 mock tests per week (about 1 per day — 28 total) to build toward a passing score of 60 or higher on the official written exam (학과시험).

Mind Education, Every Session

A compulsory part of every class, run in partnership with the International Mind Education Institute (IMEI), focused on emotional resilience, self-discipline, and responsible attitudes behind the wheel — because safe driving starts with a steady mindset.

Good to Know Before You Enroll

  • All academy payments are non-refundable, including membership, enrollment, and installment payments.
  • Government, examination, and third-party fees are separate from academy fees and are set by the relevant authorities.
  • The academy provides education, training, and guidance, but cannot guarantee that any applicant will pass an examination or receive a license — final decisions rest with Korean government authorities.
  • Remedial training is available as needed, at no fixed limit, until a student is ready to pass their current stage.
Official Resources

Important Driving Sites in Korea

Official government sources to double-check requirements, fees, and procedures alongside your KDA classes.

KOROAD — Driver's License Acquisition Guide

The Korea Road Traffic Authority's official English guide to the full license process: written test, functional test, road test, required documents, and fees.

Visit safedriving.or.kr

KOROAD — Foreign License Exchange Guide

Official English guidance on exchanging a foreign driver's license for a Korean one: eligible countries, required documents, and current fees.

Visit safedriving.or.kr

Safe Driving Integrated Civil Petition Portal

도로교통공단's main portal for booking exams, checking test center locations, and other driver's license civil services (Korean).

Visit safedriving.or.kr

DFA Apostille Application & Appointment System

The Philippine Department of Foreign Affairs' official portal for booking and processing Apostille authentication, needed for license conversion.

Visit apostille.gov.ph
