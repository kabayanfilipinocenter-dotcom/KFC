  Driving Class

Learn, prepare, and become a confident driver in South Korea. From road rules to practical driving skills, KFC helps you get behind the wheel with confidence.

What We Offer

Driving Class Overview

Everything you need to prepare for driving in South Korea, guided by people who understand the journey.

Written Test Preparation

Guided study sessions covering Korean traffic rules, road signs, and the topics covered on the written driving exam.

Practical Driving Guidance

Tips and orientation on Korean road conditions, driving etiquette, and what to expect during practical evaluation.

Language Support

Explanations in Filipino and English alongside Korean terms, so language is never a barrier to learning.

License Process Help

Guidance on the documents and steps involved in applying for and converting a driver's license in Korea.

Small Group Learning

Learn alongside fellow Kabayans in a supportive, encouraging environment built for real progress.

Flexible Scheduling

Class times arranged around work schedules, so preparing for your license fits into your life in Korea.

Getting Started

How the Program Works

A simple, guided path from first inquiry to confident driving.

1

Reach Out to KFC

Message or call the KFC team to express interest and ask about the current class schedule.

2

Join an Orientation

Attend a short orientation to understand the licensing process and what the class will cover.

3

Attend Study Sessions

Work through written test material and road rules together with fellow classmates.

4

Prepare for Practical Evaluation

Receive guidance on what to expect and how to approach the practical driving assessment.
